# Iteration 023: rock, sun glitter and broadleaf shapes

Scope: points 2–4 of [v0.1.2](../reviews/v0.1.2.md), carried forward in [v0.2.0](../reviews/v0.2.0.md). Current brief: [Milestone 1.5](../briefs/BRIEF-1.5.md).

## Changes

- Replace the single pale rock colour with slate, warm stone and weathered mineral variation in TSL. Shade crevices using concavity measured at two radii in the existing heightmap. The height-driven rock blend starts higher, keeping green shoulders below exposed peaks.
- Narrow the sun-glitter response and reduce its strength from 2.5 to 0.75. Reduce the broad highlight contribution as well. Keep the animated wave spectrum, shoreline foam, shallow-water colours and sun direction.
- Use three broadleaf crown families: spreading, upright multi-stem and asymmetric. Add forks and branches, uneven crown lobes, cluster colour variation and per-tree width/depth variation derived from existing seeded attributes. Merge each template into a bark mesh and foliage mesh before instancing.
- World generation, tree placement, startup deadlines and fallback handling are unchanged. No dependencies were added.

## Validation

- `npm run build`: pass, with the existing bundle-size advisory.
- `npm test`: all 20 tests pass.
- Screenshot batch: seeds 1, 42, 7, 99 and 2026, plus forced WebGL2 for seed 1. Images use the normal headless command and retain both HUD labels. See [capture metadata](023-captures.json).
- Opened and inspected all six images together, with iteration 022 seed 42 as the before-image. All six show a complete scene and resolved WebGL2/FPS labels. No startup workaround flags were used.
- Rock: the pale uniform cap is replaced by muted warm/cool stone faces and dark channels following terrain hollows. Seed 42 shows the clearest before/after difference; seed 2026 shows warm lit crests and cooler crevices across a larger foreground peak. Green shoulders remain below the exposed rock.
- Glitter: the broad bright stripe is now a narrower, broken line of smaller reflections. Blue water remains visible between highlights; the sun disc, waves and shoreline foam remain visible across seeds.
- Broadleaf: spreading, taller and asymmetric crowns are visibly mixed through the groves. Uneven lobes and visible forks replace the repeated round ball on a straight pole. Seed 2026 provides the clearest foreground view; the small seed 99 islet also retains varied crown outlines.
- Forced WebGL2 matches the default-path appearance for seed 1, allowing for animated wave/foam phase.
- The trees remain stylised procedural vegetation. Native WebGPU and real-hardware FPS are still unverified; these images use software WebGL2.
