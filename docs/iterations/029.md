Simulation time is held during preparation, hidden tabs and graphics recovery; these presentation holds do not advance or alter simulation state.

Replay reconstructs the seed and initial dials, applies interventions at their original ticks and verifies the full regenerated event stream. Snapshots and exported logs are detached copies.
