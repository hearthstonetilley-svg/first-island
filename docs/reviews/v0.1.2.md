# Review of dd061d6 (round 5): CHANGES REQUESTED (one blocker), visuals accepted

Visually a big leap and it meets the look in REVIEW-3: real relief, clearly different silhouettes per seed (1 = two
islands, 7 = long ridge with a bay, 2026 = volcano with an islet), turquoise shallows, foam line, sun glitter, blue sky,
sensible tree zoning. 7 tests pass.

## Blocker
1. START-UP HANG (regression). src/render/scene.ts now does a bare `await renderer.init()`. When the WebGPU adapter
   request stalls, the page stays on "starting renderer..." forever. Reproduced 3 times in 4 headless loads, on seeds 1
   and 99 (not seed specific; generation itself takes ~70 ms for every seed tested). Round 4 had a timeout fallback;
   it was removed. Fix: race init against a timeout (about 4 s); on timeout or error, build a fresh renderer with
   forceWebGL: true. If that also fails, show a clear message in the label instead of hanging. Add a unit test for the
   fallback decision logic (pure function), and load each of seeds 1, 42, 7, 99 three times headless to prove no hang.
