# Astra audit 16 (GPT-6-Astra), 19 Oct 2026: v0.10.0, boats and the islet (PR #30)

Brief: adversarial review of boats, the sea and the islet against the item ledger, save and resume, the startup and performance contract and the rule that boats are found, not given. Run visibly in tmux session island, window audit, with codex exec, read-only.

## Verdict, verbatim

ready to leave draft: no

1. **[P2] Moored craft are not synchronized with the renderer.** [server/sandbox.ts:356 emits `landfall`, but the client adds structures only for `built` events. New arrivals remain invisible, including after graphics recovery. Conversely, launching a craft removes it server-side without notifying the client, so a craft loaded from `hello` remains visibly moored after departure.

2. **[P2] Relaunching craft causes duplicate structure IDs.** [server/sandbox.ts:351 assigns `structures.length + 1`, while launching splices existing entries. Reproduced: boat #1 lands, hut #2 is built, boat #1 departs and lands again as #2. The saved structures now contain duplicate IDs; the client’s keyed map overwrites one, and the renderer rejects the second.

3. **[P2] Voyages can pass underneath the main island.** [server/sandbox.ts:550 connects any shore directly to the destination without checking intervening terrain. On seed 1, launching from `(14, -0.4)` crosses terrain of height `2.00` at 10% progress, while `stepVoyages` places the rider at `y = 0.05`. The voyage needs a sea-clear route or a valid departure restriction.

4. **[P2] The director gives main-island agents the islet-exclusive resource.** [src/sandbox/realworld.ts:27 adds obsidian as tier zero, but `direct()` selects from all tier-zero substances without checking `islet`. An in-memory probe reproduced a main-island obsidian gift. This bypasses the crossing requirement; the eval checks gathering only and misses this source.

5. **[P2] The islet-rendering eval passes without rendering an islet.** [scripts/eval-sandbox.mjs:391 checks `isletDrawn()`, which returns only `!!isle`. Removing the islet’s `addTerrain` call would leave this check passing. The assertion needs evidence of rendered geometry or pixels.

Client and server typechecks passed. Targeted probes ran without model calls or file changes. Vitest was blocked by its config-loader write on the read-only filesystem; browser checks were not run.

## What was done

1. Landfall and leaving a mooring now reach the page (a landfall carries the moored craft; taking a craft from its mooring sends an unmoored event), and the renderer can remove a structure. A new eval check sails a moored raft back and requires the page to draw exactly the craft the island has.
2. Structure ids only grow (the next id is one above the highest), so a craft that leaves and lands again never reuses an id. The same check requires unique ids.
3. A voyage needs a sea-clear course (seaClear in src/sandbox/sea.ts, unit-tested): the landing is the nearest far shore a straight course reaches over water only; from a shore with land in the way the rider learns that and stays. Under test, a rider over land at sea is reported, and the eval fails on any report.
4. The director washes ashore only what belongs to the agent's island (shoreGifts, unit-tested): never obsidian on the main island.
5. The islet check now reads pixels: with the clock held at noon, the camera looks at the islet (more than 30 percent land) and at open sea well beyond it (under 10 percent). Its first runs exposed a real flaw from the day cycle: with the sun overhead, sun glints sat under a downward view as white blots; glints now fade as the sun climbs (unit-tested).

Local after the fixes: unit tests 118/118, sandbox eval 89/89, zero model calls.
