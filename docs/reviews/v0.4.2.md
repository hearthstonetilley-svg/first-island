# Review of round 13 (iterations 034 to 037): CHANGES REQUESTED

## What this round fixed
- **Extinction, with a measured cause.** Before changing anything the builder instrumented every reproduction
  condition (035): the collapse was inadequate replacement breeding (a rest threshold above the point where agents
  start resting, and an energy threshold that excluded most of each cohort), followed by mates thinning out as
  cohorts died together. The fix (036) followed the measurements.
- **Exact replay across processes and from Node to Chromium** (034, 037): full-state replay hashes, world changes
  logged, pause and speed excluded, trees and fruit owned by the simulation and rendered from its snapshots. This is
  the foundation minds will stand on.
- **Pace near one generation dial.** Reviewer's own measurement from the event log over 21,600 ticks: mean parent age
  1.03 dials (seed 1), 1.05 (seed 42), 1.05 (seed 2026). The 30-minute pace (decision 20) now holds.

## Reviewer corrections, on the record
1. Round 12's review said biological generations ran about three times too fast. That came from the maturity age
   alone and was overstated: the builder's diagnosis measured mean parent age at 0.7 to 0.96 dials then.
2. The viability band in round 12's review (final third between 0.5x and 1.5x the founder count) is withdrawn. It tied
   viability to the number of founders, not to what an island can feed. The builder implemented the 21,600-tick
   horizon and the grandchild rule but not the band; it is not enforced because it was wrong, not because it was skipped.

Starvation is 1 to 2 percent of deaths. Two seeds sit at the 200 cap and two are still climbing: the cap, not the
island, is limiting the population. Survival is meant to be the engine (decision 14), and minds on this island
would have nothing to decide. Next: docs/briefs/BRIEF-3.5-carrying-capacity.md, a response-based test (the food dial
must move the equilibrium; the cap must never be reached), queued before the minds brief.
