## Astra audit (docs/reviews/astra-audit-2026-10-18.md): status of each finding
1. Recovery could strand the owner with adaptation off. **Fixed**: a live loading screen is made for the replacement,
   the owner stays entered with quality adapting at once, the camera pose and every structure survive. Not covered by
   the eval: headless WebGL2 cannot lose a device.
2. Low quality still heavy. **Partly fixed**: an opt-in minimal tier (no post-processing, smaller shadows) and a cheaper
   overlay. The software renderer stays near 1 fps even there, so the next cuts wait for a real-GPU reading.
3. The web redrew everything every frame. **Fixed**: a static layer redrawn only on change; 0 draws in 5 s when idle.
4. The eval proved nothing about performance and could spend. **Fixed in part**: zero calls enforced, quality must keep
   sampling and must step down to minimal on a slow renderer, camera pose checked in full. Still one renderer mode.
5. Councils bypassed limits and Pause. **Fixed**: one limit for every call, councils stop on Pause or an empty room,
   no call for a part with too few items, Codex cost labelled not measured.
6. Reload flashing. **Fixed**: dark page from the first frame; the toolbar stays hidden until entry; no box animation.
7. Camera ownership. **Fixed**: Free, drag, wheel and keys end any follow; Island is the default.
8. Fact tracing did not isolate. **Fixed**: one link per mind that knows the selected fact (checked by the eval).
9. Layout clipped and reserved hidden panels. **Fixed**: insets from measured panels; controls whole at 1400, 1200, 900 px.
10. Thoughts out of order. **Fixed**: arrivals land oldest first and an older thought never overwrites a newer one.
