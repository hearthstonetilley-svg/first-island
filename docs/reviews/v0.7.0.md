## Astra audit (docs/reviews/astra-audit-2026-10-18.md): status of each finding
1. Recovery could strand the owner with adaptation off. **Fixed**: a live loading screen is made for the replacement,
   the owner stays entered with quality adapting at once, the camera pose and every structure survive. Not covered by
   the eval: headless WebGL2 cannot lose a device.
2. Low quality still heavy. **Partly fixed**: an opt-in minimal tier (no post-processing, smaller shadows) and a cheaper
   overlay. The software renderer stays near 1 fps even there, so the next cuts wait for a real-GPU reading.
3. The web redrew everything every frame. **Fixed**: a static layer redrawn only on change; 0 draws in 5 s when idle.
4. The eval proved nothing about performance and could spend. **Fixed in part**: zero calls enforced, quality must keep
   sampling and must step down to minimal on a slow renderer, camera pose checked in full. Still one renderer mode.
5. Councils bypassed limits and Pause. **Fixed**: one limit for every call, councils stop on Pause or an empty room,
   no call for a part with too few items, Codex cost labelled not measured.
6. Reload flashing. **Fixed**: dark page from the first frame; the toolbar stays hidden until entry; no box animation.
7. Camera ownership. **Fixed**: Free, drag, wheel and keys end any follow; Island is the default.
8. Fact tracing did not isolate. **Fixed**: one link per mind that knows the selected fact (checked by the eval).
9. Layout clipped and reserved hidden panels. **Fixed**: insets from measured panels; controls whole at 1400, 1200, 900 px.
10. Thoughts out of order. **Fixed**: arrivals land oldest first and an older thought never overwrites a newer one.

## Second Astra audit (commit 316a71b): NOT READY, then fixed

Verbatim in docs/reviews/astra-audit-2-2026-10-18.md. It found the first review's "accepted" overstated closure. Fixed in
iteration 057:
1. The web created a repeating timer every frame in debug mode. Now one stable debug object, nothing per frame.
2. A malformed model reply could crash the server. Every decision is now coerced to a safe shape before anything acts,
   and each agent's task is contained; the eval feeds deliberately malformed replies.
3. Hypothesis marks were unsound ("not cord" scored right when cord was made, "rope" scored wrong). Predictions are now
   read with negation, whole-word matching and everyday aliases (src/sandbox/hypothesis.ts, unit-tested on the audit's
   cases). The score is labelled agreement with the island's rules; a real thing with no recipe is "untested", never wrong.
4. Tries without a prediction were silently left out. They are now counted as missing and shown; scripted strikes, skill
   steps and council parts carry predictions.
5. Council budget could be overspent in a race, and councils acted after Pause. Budget is reserved at launch, after any
   wait; Pause adjourns a council in progress (checked by the eval).
6. A drag during a settled group follow did not take the camera. Any drag or wheel now hands the camera to the owner.
7. "Minimal" was shown before the renderer switched. The readout shows the quality actually applied; only the latest
   request can switch when its compile finishes.
Also: thought packets are bounded, fact positions stay put as the core grows, and the layout holds at 640 px.
The CI eval now waits for the machine's own speed (GitHub's runner drew one frame every 2.7 s).

Evidence after the fixes: sandbox eval 41/41, unit tests 86.
