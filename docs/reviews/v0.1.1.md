# Review of 5eb110b (Milestone 1.5): better, still not the target

FPS counter: present top right, good. (Headless runs on a software GPU, so its fps numbers mean nothing; judge fps on real hardware.)
Target remains: the polished stylised-realistic look of current high-end Three.js WebGPU showcases. It currently looks
like a toy diorama. Specific failures, with the approach to use:

1. FLAT PLATE. The island is a flat green disc on a raised sand rim. No hills.
   Give it real relief: one main peak or ridge per island (ridged multifractal or a seeded volcano cone plus fbm),
   peak height roughly 4x the current, valleys, gentle lowlands near the coast, a narrow beach band only at the
   waterline. Rock appears on the steep upper slopes.
2. SAME SILHOUETTE FOR EVERY SEED (third time). Seeds 1, 42, 7 are the same blob.
   Build the land mask from 2 to 4 seeded blobs (random centre, radius, elongation) unioned, then domain-warp the
   distance field with fbm. Some seeds should give a crescent, a long island, two islands joined by a sand spit, a
   small offshore islet. Add a test that compares the land masks of seeds 1, 42, 7 and requires each pair to differ
   in at least 25% of land cells.
3. GHOST SLAB UNDER WATER. A big dark rectangular underwater plateau with hard edges fills the frame.
   The seabed must slope smoothly from the shore down to deep water and keep going down past the visible area;
   water opacity and colour must increase with depth so the deep seabed is invisible. No visible square anywhere.
4. TREES LOOK LIKE TOYS. Pure black trunks; palms everywhere; trees too big for the island.
   Brown trunks with shading. Palms only on and just behind the beach, broadleaf in lowlands and valleys, pines on
   upper slopes. Scale trees down to roughly half, and use more of them in natural groves with meadows between.
   Slight per-instance hue and lean.
5. WATER READS AS FOG. No visible waves or glints.
   Visible wave motion near the camera, a bright foam line hugging the shore, sun specular highlights, clear
   turquoise in the shallows over sand. Reduce the grey haze so the ocean colour shows.
6. SKY IS GREY. Blue gradient sky, visible sun disc with soft glow, warm sunlight, cooler shadow fill.
7. CAMERA. Start closer and lower so the island fills about 60% of the frame, horizon in the upper third.
8. POST: add subtle bloom on sun glints and ambient occlusion if the TSL post-processing nodes make it cheap.
   Keep it smooth on a normal desktop GPU.

against every point above, honestly. Build and tests pass. Commit locally. No remote.
